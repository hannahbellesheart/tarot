This directory contains placeholder structure for tarot card images.

Directory Structure:
/images/
  /major/          - Major Arcana cards (00-21)
  /cups/           - Cups suit cards
  /wands/          - Wands suit cards  
  /swords/         - Swords suit cards
  /pentacles/      - Pentacles suit cards

Image Naming Convention:
- Major Arcana: 00-the-fool.jpg, 01-the-magician.jpg, etc.
- Minor Arcana: ace-of-cups.jpg, two-of-cups.jpg, etc.

You can replace these placeholder paths with your own tarot card images.
The app is designed to gracefully handle missing images by hiding them and showing card names instead.
